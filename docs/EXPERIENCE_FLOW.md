# Experience Flow — GHELI (Version 4: The Art of Being You)

**Recipient**: Shruti ("Gheli")  
**Occasion**: Birthday — 25 November 2026  
**Creative Principle**: *Show, don't explain.* Twelve connected, visually distinct chapters structured like an interactive short film.

---

## Chapter Sequence & Transition Choreography

| # | Scene ID | Title | Visual Identity & Core Interaction | Exit / Connecting Transition |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 01 | `BLACK_INTRO` | The Black-Screen Introduction | Pure `#000000` viewport, zero chrome. Sequential editorial typography beats: *"Hey, Gheli."* → *"I made something for you."* → *"It's a little different."* → *"Ready?"* | Subtle scale & opacity dissolve into Spider-Man canvas. |
| 02 | `SPIDERMAN` | Spider-Man | Authentic Turtle-Graphics contour canvas (`bhadwikabday-main/experiences/spiderman/`). Live pen stroke + SVG turtle cursor drawing silhouette, tap to fast-forward. 4 short hopeful text beats after sketch. | Silhouette fades cleanly into kinetic dark plum space. |
| 03 | `GIRL_BEHIND_NAME` | The Girl Behind the Name | Kinetic typography portrait. *"But that's Gheli."* centerpiece with interactive `G H E L I` letters responding to pointer/touch (tilt, bounce, scatter & reassemble). | Letters drift outward into floating timeline markers. |
| 04 | `TWO_YEARS_AGO` | Somewhere, Two Years Ago | Minimalist nostalgic timeline (`25 NOV 2024` → `25 NOV 2026`) with subtle ruled-notebook line & page-turn motion. Zero fabricated facts. | Page sweep into the Instagram phone entrance. |
| 05 | `INSTAGRAM_ARCHIVE` | Instagram Conversation Archive | **Two strictly separated parts**:<br>1. **Part One ("Where it all began.")**: Casa-Tanvi-inspired OLED phone & DM animation reconstructing `25–26 NOV 2024` first chat (`chats/1000019990, 1992, 1989.jpg`) + authentic screenshot toggle.<br>2. **Part Two ("And then, the little moments.")**: Individual reveal of all 5 `chats/moments/*.jpg` screenshots at native aspect ratio. | Screenshots dissolve into glowing particles/petals rising into the garden. |
| 06 | `FLOWER_GARDEN` | The Flower Garden | Full-viewport animated botanical meadow (`animated-flower` + `crimson-blossom`). Staggered stems, unfolding leaves, glowing petals, 3D fluttering/landing butterflies, interactive blossom tap bursts & pointer fireflies. | Garden dims softly as luminous memories drift forward. |
| 07 | `MEMORY_CONSTELLATION` | Memory Constellation | Spatial floating gallery of approved Shruti photographs (`pictures/*.jpg`) at varied Z-depths with orbital parallax. Click any photo to inspect full-size without obscuring faces. | Constellation converges into warm dawn paper glow. |
| 08 | `HEART_TREE` | Bow, Arrow & Heart Tree | Interactive wooden bow & winged arrow (`birthday-gift`). Drag/pull/release (mouse, touch, or keyboard) → arrow hits pulsing heart → rose flood → recursive Canvas2D heart-blossom tree + falling petals + god-rays. | Dawn glow transitions into deep plum cake spotlight. |
| 09 | `BIRTHDAY_CAKE` | The Birthday Cake | Prominent multi-tier SMIL morphing SVG cake (`birthday-cake`), 5-layer flame, user-controlled blowout, Web Audio breath + chimes, rising smoke canvas, pop-able balloons, confetti. | Candle smoke rises and clears into parchment envelope. |
| 10 | `LETTER` | A Little Note for Gheli | Interactive envelope opening + unfolding parchment letter (`content/birthday-letter.ts`). Natural, warm, slightly funny birthday note. | Screen settles and dims into quiet darkness. |
| 11 | `WRAP_UP` | Let's Wrap This Up | Slowed ambience. *"Alright, Gheli..."* → *"Let's wrap this up."* → Prominent **`LET'S WRAP THIS UP`** button. | Deep camera warp sequence launching into 3D space. |
| 12 | `MEMORY_GALAXY` | The Memory Galaxy | Three.js 3D spiral galaxy (`Galaxy-main` math) populated with crisp image sprites of Shruti's photos and chat screenshots + stardust. Camera swoop intro → full 360° OrbitControls & zoom + Reset View + Replay. | Replay resets cleanly to `BLACK_INTRO`. |
